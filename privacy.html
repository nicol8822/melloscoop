Privacy

When you allow analytics, we use Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to understand visits, scrolling, clicks and time spent in different areas of our website. Clarity also provides masked session replays and heatmaps.

Booking inputs and the estimate area are masked in session replays. Our custom analytics events record actions such as starting the booking enquiry and clicking WhatsApp; they do not send the details you type into the booking fields.

Your enquiry details are included in the WhatsApp message when you choose to continue there. Clicking the link does not automatically send the message or confirm a booking.

You can decline optional analytics or change your choice using Cookie settings in the website footer. Analytics providers may process technical data such as browser, device, approximate location and cookie identifiers under their own privacy policies.
