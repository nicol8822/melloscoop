Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the mobile dessert cart service include? +
We provide professional staff equipped with our custom mobile dessert cart and freshly prepared tiramisu directly to your event venue, delivering an immersive live-scoop experience for your guests.
How far in advance should we book the tiramisu cart? +
We recommend placing your order at least 2–3 weeks in advance to secure your preferred date, but early booking is suggested to secure your spot!
How do I confirm my final order and booking date? +
Once you select your date and check availability, you can contact us directly via the WhatsApp button at the bottom right to pay the deposit and confirm your booking.
Is the deposit refundable if the event is cancelled? +
Deposits are only refundable if cancellation is requested within 1 week after you placed your order. After this 1-week period, deposits become strictly non-refundable.
What payment methods do you accept? +
A 50% deposit is required via TNG or bank transfer to secure your event date. The remaining balance must be settled 1–2 days prior to the event.
What is the space requirement for setting up the live scoop cart? +
Our dessert cart is compact and typically requires an area of approximately 1.8m x 1.2m, making it flexible for both indoor hotel ballrooms and outdoor garden venues.
Do your service staff handle the serving process? +
Yes! Our service crew will take care of setup and serving throughout the event.
Is electricity required for the dessert cart setup? +
Yes. A standard power outlet is required to power the illuminated logo and canopy lights. Please ensure a power point is available near the setup area.
Is delivery and setup included? +
Yes! Complimentary delivery and setup are included for events in Butterworth, Perai, and Bukit Mertajam. For events outside these areas, a delivery surcharge may apply depending on the event location. We’ll confirm the exact fee with you before booking.
